🌧️ August Monsoon Advisory

August is peak monsoon season in Gujarat. Expect 30–50% longer travel times on wet roads, particularly on mountain/coastal routes (Gir, Somnath, Dwarka). Allow buffer time at each stop and check weather forecasts daily. Gujarat roads in Saurashtra region may have occasional waterlogging.
